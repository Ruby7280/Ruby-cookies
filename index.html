Our Story

Baked from a family kitchen in Kulim

Ruby Cookies started in a small home kitchen in Kulim, Kedah — one tray at a time, shared first with neighbours and friends. What began as a weekend hobby quickly became a daily ritual of measuring, mixing, and waiting by the oven door.

Today we still bake the same way: small batches, real butter, honest ingredients, and orders packed with care so every cookie arrives as good as it left the oven.
